Where are your notes?

Pick a folder of markdown notes, or an empty one to start in. gonotes works on the files in it as they are: nothing is copied or moved.

  1. Pick a vault: the folder your notes are in
  2. Type a name for a note and press Enter to write in it
  3. Press Delete to delete a note again
